About Me

I'm an Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at KIT-Kalaignar Karunanidhi Institute of Technology, currently maintaining an 8.79 CGPA. My work centers on embedded systems and IoT — from ECU architectures and communication protocols like CAN, LIN, and I2C, to building real-time edge AI systems on microcontrollers like ESP32 and STM32.

Beyond coursework, I've worked hands-on through internships studying Software Defined Vehicle concepts, and industrial training in PCB design and circuit debugging. I enjoy turning hardware and data into systems that solve real problems — whether that's predictive maintenance for industrial machines or adaptive traffic signals for emergency response.

My work on EDGE-AI Smart Auditor, a real-time power monitoring system built on STM32, has been accepted for publication at the ICOSAAS 2026 conference and will appear in IEEE Xplore.
