About Me

I'm an Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at KIT-Kalaignarkarunanidhi Institute of Technology, with a minor in Computer Science Engineering. My work sits where embedded systems, IoT, and AI meet: ECU architectures, CAN/LIN/I2C protocols, and real-time edge AI on STM32 and ESP32.

I've interned on Software Defined Vehicle concepts, trained in PCB design, and I'm now interning at Wipro 3D through NPTEL. I build systems that turn sensor data into decisions, from predictive maintenance for industrial machines to post-quantum secure payments.

My research, EDGE-AI Power Auditor: Decentralized Harmonic Analysis and NILM-Based Diagnostics, is published in IEEE Xplore.
